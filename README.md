# 📊 SalesAnalysis_ExcelProject

## OverView
This dashboard analyzes Adventure Works sales data across 4 years (2005–2008). It presents key insights about Profit, Revenue, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) in a clean, interactive layout.
It highlights Profit, Revenue, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) through a clean layout with dynamic filters for Months, Years, and Countries.
A custom VBA macro was also added to clear all filters at once, making navigation more user-friendly.

## 🚀 Features
- Interactive filters for Year, Month, and Country
- Macro-enabled button to reset all filters
- Focused views on Profitability, Customers, and Products
- Clean and easy-to-use design
  
## 🔎 Main Focus Areas
- Top 5 Profitable Products & Customers
- Top 3 Months & Weekdays generating highest profit
- Top 2 Countries contributing the most profit
- Age group contributing most to sales

## 📈 Key Insights
 - Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday contribute 43% of total profit
 - Customers aged 50+ contribute 40% of total profit
 - Products in Black, Red, and Silver generate the highest profit
 - Willie Xu is the top customer, spending the most money
 - The average customer age is 45 years
  Gender has no significant impact on profit contribution

  ## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers)
- VBA Macro for filter reset functionality
